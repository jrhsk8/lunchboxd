# Voice — user-facing copy

The tone every user-facing string keeps. Sourced from `src/`, authoritative as of 2026-07-26. The site is a Letterboxd riff where the subject matter is edible; the copy is in on the joke without winking at it.

## The register

Deadpan-playful, food-flavored, never corporate. The joke is treating lunch with cinephile gravity: "Every bite goes on the record", "the crowd has spoken", "like Letterboxd, but you can eat the subject matter". Feature copy states the mechanic plainly and lets the premise carry the humor.

## Rules

- **Plain words, full sentences.** Explain mechanics simply ("Categories belong to everyone. Invent one, score what you eat out of five…"). No jargon, no fake enthusiasm, no exclamation points.
- **Empty states get personality**: a bold matter-of-fact line plus one playful sub-line ("Nothing ranked yet" / "The first bite is yours." · "No one by that handle" / "'x' hasn't pulled up a chair here."). One joke per state, not two.
- **Warnings are honest and specific.** The guest sign-out warning says exactly what is lost and what survives. Never soften a destructive action's copy for tone.
- **Placeholders teach by example**: "hotdog_hank", "Costco slice, leftover pad thai…", "Pizza, Gas Station Sushi, Soup-Adjacent…". Examples do the explaining. The same trick carries the guest naming — "a serial number — guest-4f2a1 and the like" says it faster than a rule would.
- **Letterboxd vocabulary where it maps**: hearts are "loved it", the mark's meaning is score-independent. Don't invent parallel terms for concepts Letterboxd already named.
- **Buttons are short verbs**: "Log it", "Start ranking", "Add email", "Sign out". **Clarity outranks brevity** where the short verb hides the mechanic — auth buttons spell out what happens ("Send sign-in link", not "Email link"), owner-ruled 2026-07-24. Conventional beats clever on anything that gates access.

## Terms

The vocabulary lives in [glossary.md](glossary.md) and binds every surface, not only copy. Two things about _how the words sound_ stay here, because they are tone rather than terminology:

- **Letterboxd vocabulary where it maps.** Hearts are "loved it"; don't invent a parallel term for a concept Letterboxd already named.
- **The mechanic, in the reader's words.** A category is _invented_, a ranking is _logged_, a handle is _picked_ — verbs a person would use about themselves.
